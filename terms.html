The service

Tankfull is a free app, run by Adam Hunter, a sole trader in Northern Ireland, that shows fuel prices, EV chargers and estimated savings in Northern Ireland. It is currently a beta: a test version that may have bugs, change, or be unavailable at times.

Prices and information

  • Fuel prices come from forecourts through the UK Government's Fuel Finder scheme. We show them as reported and never change them, but they can be wrong or out of date. Always check the price at the pump or charger before you fill up.
  • Charger details come from OpenStreetMap, Open Charge Map and charging networks, and may be incomplete. Check that a charger fits your vehicle and is working before relying on it.
  • "Worth the drive" savings, opening hours and journey figures are estimates based on the information you give and the data available.
  • To report a wrong fuel price, use the Fuel Finder service.

Using Tankfull safely and fairly

  • Don't use the app while driving. Set it up before you set off, or let a passenger use it.
  • Follow the law and the rules at forecourts and charging sites.
  • Don't try to copy the app's data in bulk, interfere with the service, or get around its security.
