Privacy policy

Short version: no account, no adverts, no tracking. Your location is rounded and never stored. We only keep what's needed to send the price alerts you set up, and you can delete that at any time.

Last updated 31 October 2026

Who we are

Tankfull is an app and website run by Adam Hunter, a sole trader in Northern Ireland ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described here.

Contact: tankfull.app@icloud.com

What we collect and why

1. Your location, when you allow it

Tankfull uses your phone's location to find fuel stations and chargers near you and to work out the drive. Before it leaves your phone, the location is rounded to about 100 metres. Our database uses it to answer that one question and does not store it, and it isn't linked to you or your phone. If you don't allow location, Tankfull shows Coleraine and you can search instead.

When you search for a town or postcode, your phone's own map service (Apple Maps on iPhone) looks it up, and the result is used in the same way.

Lawful basis: our legitimate interest in showing you the nearby results you've asked for. You control location access in your phone's settings.

2. Price alerts, only if you set one up

To send a notification, our server has to know where to send it and what you asked for. When you turn on alerts we store:

  • a random code made by the app for your phone (it doesn't contain any personal details), your phone's notification token and its type (for example iOS)
  • each alert's settings: fuel type, your target price, the station or the area (its centre rounded to about 1 km, the place name and the distance), whether you only want "worth the drive" deals, and the vehicle figures used for that (usual fill in litres, town and motorway mpg)
  • when and at what price we last notified you, and when the alert and the phone were last active

Lawful basis: providing the alert service you've asked for (contract).

3. Things that stay on your phone

Your vehicles, favourites, settings and the last prices you looked at are saved only on your phone. We never receive them. They are deleted when you delete the app.

4. Emails you send us

If you email us, we use your email address and message to reply and to fix the problem. Lawful basis: our legitimate interest in answering you.

5. Technical logs

Like any online service, our providers automatically record basic details of requests to the database and website (such as the IP address and time) for security and to fix faults. We don't use these to identify you, track you, or build a profile.

Lawful basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and working.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, names, phone numbers or email sign-ups in the app.
  • No adverts, no advertising IDs, no analytics or tracking tools, and no cookies on this website.
  • We never sell or share your data for marketing, and we don't make automated decisions about you.

Where data goes outside the UK, it's protected by the safeguards UK data protection law requires, such as an adequacy decision or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum in the provider's data processing terms.

Apple also has its own privacy policy for TestFlight, the App Store and notifications: apple.com/legal/privacy.

How long we keep it

  • Location: not kept.
  • Alerts: until you delete them, or tap Delete my alerts and data. We also clear phones automatically: 30 days after notifications stop working (for example, the app was deleted), 90 days after last use if there are no alerts, and in any case after 24 months without use.
  • Emails: up to 12 months after your question is sorted.
  • Technical logs: a short time, usually days, under our providers' own retention settings.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive a copy.

Because we don't know who you are, the quickest way to delete everything we hold is in the app: tap the i button at the top, then Delete my alerts and data. For anything else, email tankfull.app@icloud.com and we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, phone 0303 123 1113.

Children

Tankfull is made for drivers and isn't aimed at children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change matters.
