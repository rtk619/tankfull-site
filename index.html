Support

Questions, problems or ideas: email tankfull.app@icloud.com. We aim to reply within a few days.

A fuel price is wrong

Prices come straight from forecourts through the government's Fuel Finder scheme, and we never change them. To get a wrong price corrected, report it to the Fuel Finder service. You can also do this from any station page in the app ("Price looks wrong? Report it").

A charger is missing or wrong

Email us with the charger's name and town and what's wrong. Charger locations come from OpenStreetMap and Open Charge Map, and anyone can correct those too.

Delete your data

In the app, tap the i button at the top, then Delete my alerts and data. Everything else Tankfull keeps is on your own phone and goes when you delete the app. See the privacy policy.

Where the data comes from

  • Fuel prices, forecourt details, facilities and opening hours: UK Government Fuel Finder scheme. Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
  • Extra station facilities and charger locations: © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence.
  • Charger locations and connectors: Open Charge Map contributors (CC BY 4.0) and the data providers credited on each charger.
  • Charger prices and live status, when shown: the charging network's own open data, under the Open Government Licence.

Prices and savings are a guide. Always check the price at the pump or charger before you fill up.
